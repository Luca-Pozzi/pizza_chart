About

The pizza_chart project began in late 2024 at B12 building of Polo Territoriale di Lecco, Politecnico di Milano as a lighthearted tool to visualize stats from our pizza days at work.
The project escalated a bit as few data analysis tools have been added and this webpage released.

If you can’t enjoy pizza with us, we hope you can at least enjoy our pizza charts!
